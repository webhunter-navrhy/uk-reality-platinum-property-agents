Nestled in the vibrant and sought-after area of St. John's, Worcester, this charming one-bedroom duplex flat offers a unique opportunity for those seeking a well-located and characterful home. Positioned on the first floor, directly above a local optician, the property benefits from a prime spot within this bustling community, providing easy access to local amenities and transport links.

Upon entering, you are greeted by a thoughtfully laid out living space. The flat features a comfortable lounge, perfect for relaxation and entertaining, which enjoys pleasant views over the historic St. John's Church Yard, adding a touch of tranquillity to urban living. The well-appointed kitchen provides a functional space for culinary pursuits with space for dining, designed to maximise efficiency and convenience.

The property boasts a generously sized double bedroom, offering a peaceful retreat at the end of the day. Adjacent to this, the bathroom is well-maintained and provides all necessary facilities. As a duplex flat, it offers a distinct layout that enhances the sense of space and privacy within the property.

One of the key considerations for this property is the lease. Currently, there are 64 years remaining on the lease. However, prospective buyers will be pleased to note that there is potential for a lease extension the details of which are to be confirmed, which will significantly enhance the long-term appeal and value of the flat. A lease shorter than 70 years can affect the number of mortgage lenders that are willing to lend however our Mortgage Adviser* can assist with reviewing the options.

St. John's itself is a highly desirable area, known for its friendly atmosphere, independent shops, cafes, and excellent local services. The proximity to St. John's Church Yard offers a picturesque outlook and a sense of community. Worcester city centre is also easily accessible, providing a wider array of shopping, dining, and cultural attractions, including the famous Worcester Cathedral and the scenic River Severn.

This property is offered with a guide price of £110,000, representing an attractive entry point into the Worcester property market. It is an ideal purchase for first-time buyers, individuals looking to downsize, or investors seeking a property with strong rental potential in a popular location. Early viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate the charm, location, and potential this delightful duplex flat has to offer.
